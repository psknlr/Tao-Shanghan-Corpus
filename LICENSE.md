## Source texts
`02_source_texts/` contains historical works whose textual content is in the public domain by
age. The digital transcriptions were obtained from 中醫典籍資料庫 (Database of Chinese Medical Classics), jicheng.tw (https://jicheng.tw/tcm/index.html). The terms of use of
that repository are `not_stated_in_source` in the materials available to the dataset authors; users who intend
to redistribute the transcriptions should consult the source repository directly. The works
themselves are pre-modern and not under copyright.

## Disclaimer
This is a historical-literature dataset. It is not medical advice and must not be used as a
source of clinical guidance.
