## Legends

**Fig. 1 | Construction and provenance of the corpus.** **a**, Construction workflow. The 57 catalogued historical works (425 plain-text files, 4.76 million characters, Eastern Han to Qing) were retrieved from the Database of Chinese Medical Classics (jicheng.tw) and assigned an evidence role. The two canonical texts were segmented into 681 clause records (398 numbered canonical clauses and 283 auxiliary clauses), and the two variant editions and nine commentaries were aligned to the canonical clauses (616 variant readings and 2,958 commentary records). The other 44 works are catalogued and released as full text but are not aligned in this release. The clause, commentary and variant records are the 4,255 nodes of a relation graph with 4,286 typed edges in eight relation types, and are also released as 4,255 unified text records. Validation: all six structured layers pass schema validation, with no duplicate identifiers and no orphan edge endpoints; all 4,255 records trace to a catalogued work and a source file; and all 425 files match their SHA-256 checksums. Outline colours are used in all figures: black, canonical text; vermillion, variant witnesses; blue, commentaries; grey, other material (dashed where not aligned). **b**, Provenance chain of one record, canonical clause 23 (SHL_SONGBEN_0023). The record carries the SHA-256 hash of its text. Its source_id resolves to the catalogued work SHSRC0033, whose only source file is listed in the file manifest with a checksum that matches SHA256SUMS.txt; the upstream repository is recorded in the provenance table. Hashes are truncated for display.

**Fig. 2 | Composition of the source catalogue and of the canonical clause layer.** **a**, The 57 catalogued works by period, as recorded in the catalogue, and by length (top; characters per work, log scale), with the total length of each period (bottom). Each circle is one work; horizontal offsets only separate overlapping circles. Enlarged circles mark the 13 works that feed the structured layers: the canonical texts (black; C, clause edition; S, Song edition), the variant witnesses (vermillion; G, Gui edition; Q, Qianjin Yifang) and the nine clause-aligned commentaries (blue), numbered in order of composition as in Figs. 3 and 4. Grey circles are the other 44 works. **b**, Annotation of the 398 canonical clauses by section of the six-channel division, in canonical order. Bars give the clauses in each section. Cells give the percentage of a section's clauses that have at least one value in each of the 15 list-valued annotation fields of the clause record, grouped as clinical findings, treatment, course and textual features; shading repeats the cell value. Annotation values were extracted automatically during construction. Relapse denotes the section on yin–yang exchange and relapse after recovery (陰陽易差後勞復病). n = 57 works (a) and 398 clauses (b).

**Fig. 3 | Record structure and linkage.** **a**, Data model of the release. Boxes are record types, with record counts, source directory and principal fields; arrows run from a foreign key to the record it references. Each relation joins two nodes, and each node is one clause, commentary or variant record (node_id equals the record identifier); commentary and variant records reference both a canonical clause (clause_id) and a catalogued work (source_id). **b**, Edges by relation type (log scale), coloured by the type of target record: commentary (blue), variant (vermillion) or clause (black). **c**, Every record linked to canonical clause 23. Left, the 11 commentary records aligned to the clause, from eight works, numbered as in Fig. 2a; the open circle marks the commentary without a record for this clause (Shanghan Guanzhu Ji). Right, the two variant readings, with their similarity to the canonical clause in parentheses, and the clauses joined to clause 23 by sequence (solid) and differential (dashed) relations. The box gives the first sentence and the closing prescription of the clause.

**Fig. 4 | Coverage and technical validation of the alignment layers.** **a**, Coverage of the 398 canonical clauses (columns, in canonical order) by each clause-aligned commentary (blue) and variant witness (vermillion). A filled cell means that at least one record of the work is aligned to the clause; numbers at right give the clauses covered. The band at top marks the sections; the histogram gives, for each clause, how many of the nine commentaries have a record for it. 396 of 398 clauses (99.5%) have at least one commentary record (median, 8 commentaries per clause); clauses 128 and 157 have none. 384 clauses have at least one variant reading. **b**, Alignment score of each commentary record against its canonical clause, by work (medians 0.85–0.94). **c**, Similarity of each variant reading to its canonical clause, by witness (medians 0.93 and 0.75); 113 of the 379 readings of the Gui edition and 9 of the 237 of the Qianjin Yifang are identical to the canonical text. Scores in b and c are those assigned by the automated alignment (range 0–1). Points are individual records, jittered vertically; boxes show the median and interquartile range, and whiskers extend to the most extreme value within 1.5 times the interquartile range. n = 2,958 commentary records (b) and 616 variant readings (c); n per work is given beside each row.
